Saigo Takamori: a tall, powerfully built male Satsuma samurai, unusually broad-shouldered and heavyset even among his peers, with the physical presence of a sumo wrestler rather than a typical swordsman — but always in normal human proportion (broad frame and stature, not an oversized head or exaggerated body scale).
Large, expressive dark eyes under thick, heavy eyebrows — famous for a gentle, almost sorrowful gaze that contrasts sharply with his imposing size.
Period-accurate sakayaki hairstyle: forehead and crown of the scalp shaved bare, remaining hair oiled and gathered into a compact chonmage topknot folded flat against the crown.
Default attire is a plain, dark-colored kimono and hakama befitting a Satsuma retainer of modest rank, with a katana and wakizashi (daisho) worn at the hip — reserve full lamellar armor only for explicit battle scenes.
Weathered, sun-browned skin from years of physical training and outdoor labor.
Bearing is calm, deeply loyal, and unhurried, masking a capacity for both fierce devotion and profound guilt beneath a gentle exterior.
