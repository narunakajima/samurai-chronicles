Sawamura Jinzaburo (Yasusuke): a low-ranking Iga retainer (musokunin, allowed a surname and a sword) of the Tsu domain
(Todo house), shown in 1853. His birth and death years are unknown and no portrait or photograph of him is known, so keep
invented details to a minimum and never show his face clearly: in boat and shore scenes he is seen from behind or with his
face in the shadow of a plain woven straw hat.
Age: shown as a mature, weathered man in his forties (an invented middle ground: the records give no age; the manga Shinobino
and the game Rise of the Ronin both present him as a veteran, older shinobi).
Build: lean, wiry, medium height; quiet, watchful bearing, economical movements; clean-shaven.
Hair: the standard Edo fashion for a man of his station: shaved pate (sakayaki) with a small topknot (chonmage) tied with
plain cord. This follows the project default; no special hair exception is needed. Under the straw hat the head is hidden.
Clothing: plain dark indigo cotton travelling clothes (kosode, short hakama or tied leggings, straw sandals) and a plain woven
straw hat; no haori (a working, discreet errand, not a formal occasion); no crest. At most one short sword at the waist and
only if the scene needs it; in the boat (sculling at the stern) no sword is visible. Dark indigo is chosen because it stays
close to the dark-clothed "ninja" image viewers expect while being ordinary period workwear.
NEVER a full black "ninja" costume: that is a stage invention (see ep063 S05). No mask, no hood, no head wrap covering the face,
no stage ninja props (no shuriken, no ninja-to, no grappling hook).
At the 1854 banquet (only as a possibility, ep123 S09): plain dark indigo kosode and hakama, bare head with sakayaki and
topknot, face turned away.
He is NOT a Tokugawa shogunate Iga doushin of Edo castle (that is a different office, see ep063 S12 and ep065 S16): he serves
the Tsu domain. Appearance confirmed in ep123 STEP 5-0 (2026-11-10).
