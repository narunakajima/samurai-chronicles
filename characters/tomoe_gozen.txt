Tomoe Gozen: athletic female warrior in her late 20s, Heian/Kamakura era.
Striking but fierce features, long dark hair often tied back beneath a helmet or worn loose beneath armor.
Wears dark lacquered samurai armor identical in function to a male commander's — no decorative or romanticized elements.
Primary weapons: naginata (long-bladed polearm) and longbow; expert horsewoman who rides powerful black warhorses.
Composed, calculating expression — calm even in chaos. Carries herself with the bearing of a commander, not a follower.
Nothing about her appearance signals "exception" or "novelty" — she belongs on the battlefield as completely as any man beside her.
