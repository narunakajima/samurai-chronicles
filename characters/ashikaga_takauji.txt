Ashikaga Takauji: tall, broad-shouldered Japanese warrior-nobleman. Early-to-mid 30s in most scenes (1333-1338); mid-40s with grey streaking his hair in later scenes (Kanno Disturbance, c.1350).
Long face, strong jaw, calm and watchful dark eyes that shift easily between deference and command.
Long black hair pulled back into a simple knot at the nape — no shaved pate. The chonmage hairstyle is anachronistic to the 14th century and must not be used.
Wears laced lamellar armor in the Nanboku-cho period o-yoroi style, dark red-brown or black lacing, marked with the Ashikaga clan crest: two black horizontal bars inside a circle (maru ni futatsu-biki). A horned kabuto helmet in battle scenes. Formal dark court robes over armor, or plain robes without armor, in ceremonial/political scenes.
Carries a long tachi sword. Bearing of restrained, deliberate authority — equally convincing kneeling before a throne or leading a cavalry charge, never visibly rushed.
Nothing European in armor, dress, or architecture around him.
