Taira no Kiyomori: commanding male court warrior-noble in his late 50s to 60s, Heian period (12th century Japan).
Stern, weathered face with sharp calculating eyes, neatly trimmed dark beard streaked with grey, an expression of absolute authority bordering on arrogance.
Wears elaborate black and deep-red Heian court robes (sokutai) with a tall black lacquered eboshi cap — aristocratic dress worn with the rigid bearing of a warrior underneath.
In war or council scenes, wears red-laced samurai armor (do-maru) marked with the Taira clan's bold butterfly (ageha-cho) crest.
Carries himself with rigid posture and quiet menace — a man utterly accustomed to obedience from everyone around him, including emperors.
