Abe Masahiro: composed, refined male shogunate chief minister (rōjū shuza) in
his early-to-mid 30s. Solidly built, full-cheeked, well-proportioned features,
calm and thoughtful eyes, a still and deliberate manner rather than a stern
or commanding one.
Traditional sakayaki hairstyle — forehead and crown shaved bare, hair oiled
and gathered into a small chonmage topknot. Formal dark kamishimo (wide-
shouldered ceremonial court dress) or plain dark kimono and hakama befitting
a senior Edo Castle administrator — never armor, never a sword drawn in
anger. Carries himself with the quiet, careful bearing of a negotiator and
builder of consensus, not a warrior.
