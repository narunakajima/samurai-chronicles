Takeda Katsuyori: muscular male warlord in his early 30s.
Strong jaw, intense dark eyes, a proud and slightly desperate bearing — always something to prove.
Wears elaborate red lacquered armor (the signature Takeda red) with a horned kabuto helmet.
Carries a long katana and commands from horseback. His posture is aggressive, leaning forward.
Handsome but strained — the face of a man who lives under the shadow of his legendary father, Takeda Shingen.
