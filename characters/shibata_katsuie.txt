Shibata Katsuie: powerfully built, barrel-chested Japanese samurai general, Sengoku period (16th century Japan), one of Oda Nobunaga's most feared senior retainers, nicknamed "Oni Shibata" (Demon Shibata) for his ferocity in battle.
As a battle-hardened field commander in his 40s-50s: thick furrowed brow, deep-set hard eyes, weathered sun-beaten face, short greying beard, traditional sakayaki shaved-forehead hairstyle with a topknot.
Wears heavy dark lacquered samurai armor (do-maru or tosei-gusoku style) with a tall, imposing kabuto helmet, bearing a simple Shibata clan crest on the breastplate and banners; broad shoulders and a thick neck convey raw physical power rather than courtly refinement.
In later-life scenes as lord of Echizen province and Kitanosho Castle (late 50s-early 60s): armor and formal robes appear more weathered and worn, greying hair and beard fuller, expression grave and resolute rather than merely fierce — a man carrying the weight of command rather than seeking glory.
Carries a katana and often a heavy iron war fan (tessen) in command scenes; in the final siege and death scenes may be shown in scorched, soot-darkened armor or plain dark formal robes, expression calm and unyielding.
