Hosokawa Tadaoki: powerful samurai lord and warrior, late 30s.
Broad-shouldered, weathered, sharply masculine features, thick brow, intense hard eyes, a short well-groomed beard/mustache. Period-accurate sakayaki hairstyle — forehead and crown shaved bare, hair gathered into a compact chonmage topknot. Wears dark lacquered armor with gold accents in battle (a refined, wealthy daimyo's war gear), or formal dark silk court robes and hakama in civilian settings. Known as both a fierce warrior and a cultivated man of taste (tea ceremony, poetry) — his bearing mixes battlefield severity with aristocratic restraint. Expression often stern, possessive, and proud.
