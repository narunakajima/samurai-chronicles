Akechi Mitsuhide: refined male samurai-scholar in his mid-50s.
High forehead, calm measured dark eyes, thin lips often pressed together in careful thought. Patrician aristocratic features, neatly groomed. Wears formal court robes in civilian settings; dark navy-and-silver armor with the kikyo (bellflower) crest in battle. Distinguished bearing — precise and cultivated. A face that reveals little, concealing deep emotion beneath a composed exterior.
