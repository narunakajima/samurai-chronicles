Tachibana Muneshige: tall, athletic male samurai general in his 20s to 30s, Sengoku period (16th century Japan), adopted heir of the Tachibana clan through marriage.
Strong jaw, confident expression, short beard.
Wears dark red and black lacquered samurai armor (do-maru) with the Tachibana clan crest (a stylized tachibana / mandarin orange blossom badge) on the breastplate and helmet.
In quiet or indoor scenes, wears simple dark kimono with minimal ornamentation.
Carries a katana and often a spear (yari) in battle scenes.
In later-life scenes, appears more weathered, with a more solemn and tempered expression.
