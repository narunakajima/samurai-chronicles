Katsu Kaishu: shrewd, calm male shogunate naval official in his late 30s to
40s. Traditional chonmage topknot with shaved pate, formal dark kamishimo or
haori attire befitting a bakufu official. Sharp, knowing eyes; composed,
almost amused demeanor even when confronted with hostility. A forward-thinking
pragmatist unafraid of Western ideas, carries himself with quiet authority
rather than samurai swagger.
