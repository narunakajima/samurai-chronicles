Oda Nobunaga: tall, imposing male warlord in his late 40s.
Sharp angular face, prominent jaw, intense piercing dark eyes that convey both intelligence and controlled menace. Period-accurate chonmage (topknot with shaved pate), clean-shaven face. Black lacquered armor with golden paulownia crest mon. Carries a European arquebus as well as a katana. Posture commanding and forward-leaning. Radiates dangerous charisma — the look of a man accustomed to being obeyed instantly.
