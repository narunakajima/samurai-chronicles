Hosokawa Gracia (born Akechi Tama, baptized Gracia): refined noblewoman, mid-to-late 30s at time of death.
Oval face, pale skin, large dark solemn eyes that hold steady rather than dart, a composed and dignified expression that rarely breaks — grief and resolve held beneath a calm surface. Long black hair worn loose and unbound (suberakashi-style), falling straight down her back and shoulders in the Momoyama-period noblewoman fashion — NOT a married woman's bound/upswept style, no sakayaki, no shaved pate. Wears rich multi-layered silk kimono (kosode) in deep reds, indigos, or gold-patterned brocade appropriate to a high-ranking daimyo's wife. As a baptized Christian she may be shown with a small European-style silver or wood crucifix or rosary held or worn discreetly against her kimono — the Christian motif integrated naturally into Momoyama-era dress, never anachronistic European clothing or architecture around her. Bearing is aristocratic, still, and controlled — a woman who has already made her peace with a hard decision.
