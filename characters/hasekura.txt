Hasekura Tsunenaga: dignified Japanese samurai diplomat in his early 40s. Lean build, composed bearing. Sharp, intelligent eyes carrying the weight of an impossible mission. Clean-shaven face, no mustache, no beard, no stubble — consistent across every scene, Japanese and European alike. Traditional early Edo-period samurai hairstyle: shaved pate (sakayaki) with a topknot (chonmage) — kept consistently even in Spanish and Roman court scenes, marking him as visibly foreign there. In Japanese contexts: black lacquered formal armor bearing the Date clan crest, or formal kamishimo/haori travel dress for non-combat scenes. In European diplomatic contexts: Japanese formal court dress with a small crucifix. Always carries sealed documents or diplomatic papers. The manner of a man performing a role he was not entirely certain he believed in — performing loyalty to a lord whose true intentions remained unclear even to him.
