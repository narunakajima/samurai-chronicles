Sasaki Kojiro: refined, aristocratic Japanese male swordsman in his late 20s.
Well-groomed appearance — elegant and composed.
Formal blue or dark blue kimono, or light ceremonial armor.
Handsome face with strong, proud features. Controlled, composed expression.
Carries an exceptionally long nodachi (longsword) — noticeably longer than a standard katana.
Posture: straight, precise, disciplined. The bearing of a court-trained master.
Every movement is deliberate and polished. A man of acknowledged status and pride.
He is the opposite of Musashi — where Musashi is raw nature, Kojiro is refined technique.
