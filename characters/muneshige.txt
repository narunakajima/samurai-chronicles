Tachibana Muneshige: tall, athletic, physically robust male samurai general in his 20s to 30s, Sengoku period (16th century Japan), adopted heir of the Tachibana clan through marriage. His surviving armor is documented as unusually heavy, consistent with a large-framed man for his era.
Strong jaw, confident expression, short beard.
Wears dark red and black lacquered samurai armor (do-maru) bearing the gyoyo crest (a paired foliate/leaf-shaped emblem inherited from the Otomo clan — NOT a citrus/mandarin-orange blossom, despite the clan name) on the breastplate and helmet. Some documented armor pieces also feature crescent-moon disc (tsukiwa) ornaments on the breastplate and helmet.
In quiet or indoor scenes, wears simple dark kimono with minimal ornamentation.
Carries a katana and often a spear (yari) in battle scenes.
In later-life scenes, appears more weathered, with a more solemn and tempered expression.
