Uesugi Kenshin: powerfully built Japanese warlord in his 30s-40s, known as the Dragon of Echigo.
Distinctive appearance: white Buddhist robes or hood worn over black lacquered armor.
Strong jaw, intense contemplative eyes combining spiritual calm and martial ferocity.
Carries an iron war fan (tessen) and sword. Prayer beads at his belt.
Commands absolute presence — a man whose faith and violence are equally absolute.
Known as Bishamonten's avatar; celibate, austere, utterly incorruptible.
Uesugi clan mon (nine-spiked roundel) visible on armor and banners.
