Yagyu Munenori: composed, watchful male samurai in his 50s-60s, lean build, sharp intelligent eyes, and a neatly trimmed greying beard and mustache.
Wears dark indigo or black formal kimono and hakama, with a small stylized geometric clan crest on the shoulder, and twin swords (katana and wakizashi) worn at the waist but rarely drawn.
Calm, controlled, almost motionless presence — radiates quiet authority and restraint rather than aggression, embodying the philosophy of "the sword that gives life."
