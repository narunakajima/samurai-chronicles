Miyamoto Musashi: lean, intense Japanese male samurai in his late 20s to early 30s.
Wild, unkempt black hair — long and loose or roughly tied.
Simple dark worn kimono, no armor — deliberate poverty of appearance.
Two swords (daisho) at his belt, or one wooden sword (bokken).
Sharp, penetrating, deeply intelligent eyes. Weathered, angular face.
Body language: coiled readiness, economy of movement.
Not aristocratic — raw, primal, utterly self-contained.
He looks like a man who has slept outdoors and won sixty duels.
