Taira no Tomomori: weathered, battle-hardened male warrior commander in his late 30s. Stern square jaw, deep-set eyes carrying quiet exhaustion and resolve. Wears dark red lacquered armor with the Taira clan's ageha-cho (swallowtail butterfly) crest. Commanding but weary bearing — a man who has led his clan through defeat after defeat and knows exactly how this will end. Carries a naginata and wears a simple iron helmet, no ornate decoration. Expression: calm, resigned dignity rather than fear.
