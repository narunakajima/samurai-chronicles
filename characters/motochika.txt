Chosokabe Motochika: lean, sharp-featured male warlord in his 40s to 50s, Sengoku period (16th century Japan), lord of Tosa province on the island of Shikoku.
Weathered face with intense dark eyes, a thin mustache and short beard, an expression that shifts between calm composure and fierce battlefield intensity.
Wears dark green and black lacquered samurai armor (do-maru) with a bold cross-shaped clan crest on the breastplate and helmet.
In quiet or indoor scenes, wears simple dark grey kimono with minimal ornamentation, reflecting a pragmatic, battle-hardened character rather than aristocratic luxury.
In early-life scenes, appears as a pale, slender youth with a withdrawn posture — transforming over the story into a hardened warrior and, in later scenes, a heavier, grief-worn presence.
