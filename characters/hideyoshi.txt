Toyotomi Hideyoshi: short, slight male figure in his 40s-50s.
Distinctive narrow, fox-like face with alert, restless eyes that miss nothing.
Contemporaries compared him to a monkey — small, wiry, energetic.
Expression shifts rapidly from warmth and charm to cold calculation.
Wears magnificent golden armor in battle scenes; formal court robes in political scenes.
Carries himself with the confident authority of a man who earned every inch of power.
In early scenes (youth): rough peasant clothing, barefoot, watchful posture of a born observer.
