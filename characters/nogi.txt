Nogi Maresuke (1849-1912): Meiji-era Imperial Japanese Army officer, born in Edo to a samurai family of the Chofu domain (a branch of Choshu). Lost his regiment's flag in the Satsuma Rebellion (1877, age 27), commanded the Third Army at the Siege of Port Arthur (1904-05), head of the Gakushuin from 1907, died on the night of Emperor Meiji's funeral (13 September 1912). Many genuine photographs survive. Appearance changes with the year, so match every scene to its date (exact facial hair per age to be confirmed in STEP 5-0).

Face and build (constant): lean and upright, a narrow, long, serious face, a straight nose, a firmly closed mouth, a grave, controlled, rarely smiling expression. Normal human proportions.

HAIR (all scenes, all ages): Meiji-era short-cropped Western-style hair fully covering the scalp. NO topknot, NO shaved crown, NO sakayaki. Deliberately NOT the Edo-period samurai look.

1877 (age 27, army major): young and lean, dark hair, dark blue Meiji army officer's tunic with black frogging, kepi-style cap, sabre, dark cloak.
1904-1906 (mid-fifties, general): weathered face, greying beard and moustache, dark general's field uniform and peaked cap, greatcoat in winter.
1907-1912 (about 58-62, head of the Gakushuin): white beard and moustache, plain dark army uniform and peaked cap.
