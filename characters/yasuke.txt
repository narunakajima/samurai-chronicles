Yasuke: a very tall Black man in his late twenties (the chronicle of 1581 says he looked 26 or 27; the diary of 1582 gives his height as about 182 cm, roughly six feet, about a head taller than the Japanese men around him), with deep, rich dark skin ("black as ink" in the diary), broad shoulders and a powerful, athletic build, in the Sengoku era a figure who stands out in every crowd. He is the only person in the episode whose appearance is deliberately not normal for the setting; show him taller than the people next to him in every shared frame.
FACE AND HAIR: No portrait survives, and no record describes his face or his hair. Provisional look (to be refined by the historian at STEP 5-0): a strong, calm, watchful face with a broad nose, a firm jaw and dark, steady eyes, clean-shaven or with only the faintest stubble. Short, close-cropped dark curly hair over the whole head. His hair is NOT shaved at the front (no sakayaki) and he has NO chonmage topknot, unlike every Japanese man around him. Prefer shots from behind, from a distance, in profile, or in deep shadow; keep any front-facing close-up to a minimum.
CLOTHING (an inference, not recorded): plain dark indigo cotton kosode and hakama, as a retainer in Nobunaga's household might wear, with one short sheathed sword (a sayamaki-style hip sword) at the left hip; no armor, no spear, no banner, no crest. Barefoot or in plain straw sandals.
Posture: upright, composed, deliberate, a man who knows he is looked at. Not a caricature: never a comic figure, never a monster, never a slave in chains; dignified and human in every shot.
