Musashibo Benkei: enormous warrior monk, towering over other men by a full head.
Broad shouldered, barrel-chested, with a shaved head and fierce commanding face. In adult scenes (the 1180s, about 30-35 years old) his shaved head is wrapped in a plain white monk's headcloth (zukin) that frames a heavy-browed, weathered face with a strong jaw; no hair, no topknot, no eboshi cap.
Wears black-lacquered haramaki armour over a dark monk's robe (saffron only in the young novice scenes). Carries a massive naginata and a bundle of tools and weapons on his back.
Fierce loyal presence — fearsome to enemies, utterly devoted to Yoshitsune. Wild warrior's eyes, but a calm, quiet gaze at rest.
