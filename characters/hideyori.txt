Toyotomi Hideyori: young male lord in his early twenties, born into the highest luxury.
Soft, handsome features with his father Hideyoshi's broad forehead and alert dark eyes.
Wears elaborate gold-and-black lacquered armor bearing the Toyotomi paulownia crest.
His posture is noble but uncertain — raised entirely within Osaka Castle's walls, he never had the chance to become the warrior his dynasty needed.
Carries the weight of Hideyoshi's impossible legacy in every expression: earnest, proud, and ultimately overwhelmed by forces beyond his control.
