Sawamura Jinzaburo (Yasusuke): a low-ranking Iga retainer of the Tsu domain (Todo house), shown in 1853, a man in his
thirties or forties. No portrait or photograph of him is known, so keep invented details to a minimum and never show
his face clearly: in boat and shore scenes he is seen from behind or with his face in the shadow of a plain woven straw hat.
Lean, wiry, medium build; quiet, watchful bearing, economical movements. Hair in the standard Edo fashion for a man of his
station: shaved pate (sakayaki) with a small topknot (chonmage) tied with plain cord; no beard.
Clothing: dark indigo cotton travelling clothes (kosode, short hakama or leggings, straw sandals) and a plain woven straw hat;
at most one short sword at the waist if the scene needs it. NEVER a full black "ninja" costume: that is a stage invention
(see ep063 S05). No mask, no hood, no crest, no stage ninja props (no shuriken, no ninja-to).
He is NOT a Tokugawa shogunate Iga doushin of Edo castle (that is a different office, see ep063 S12 and ep065 S16): he serves
the Tsu domain. Provisional file: STEP 5-0 appearance research will confirm hairstyle and dress.
