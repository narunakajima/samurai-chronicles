Minamoto no Yoshitsune: slender, graceful male warrior in his mid-20s. Delicate, almost beautiful features with sharp intense eyes — a face that conceals extraordinary martial genius. Wears elegant white and gold lacquered armor with the Minamoto clan crest (sasarindou). Moves with a dancer's precision and economy. Carries a long tachi at his hip. Expression: composed and alert, but with an undercurrent of something haunted — a man whose brilliance has always made him dangerous to those around him.
