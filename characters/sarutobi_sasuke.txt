Sarutobi Sasuke: young athletic ninja in his mid-20s, entirely legendary (not historical).
Lean, agile build suggesting extreme physical capability. Dark navy or black ninja attire.
Sharp focused eyes, determined expression. Often depicted mid-movement or mid-leap.
Associated with forest and mountain environments. Carries kunai blades.
His name means "monkey-leaping Sasuke" — his defining trait is extraordinary acrobatic ability among trees.
Serves Sanada Yukimura with absolute loyalty.
