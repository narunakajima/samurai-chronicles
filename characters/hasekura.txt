Hasekura Tsunenaga: dignified Japanese samurai diplomat in his early 40s. Lean build, composed bearing. Sharp, intelligent eyes carrying the weight of an impossible mission. In Japanese contexts: black lacquered formal armor bearing the Date clan crest. In European diplomatic contexts: Japanese formal court dress with a small crucifix. Always carries sealed documents or diplomatic papers. The manner of a man performing a role he was not entirely certain he believed in — performing loyalty to a lord whose true intentions remained unclear even to him.
