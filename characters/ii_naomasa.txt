Ii Naomasa: striking, intense male samurai general, late 30s, with sharp features, a commanding gaze, and a short dark beard/mustache.
Wears head-to-toe crimson-lacquered samurai armor (the famous "Akazonae" Red Devils armor) with black and gold trim, a golden crescent-shaped crest on his kabuto helmet, and a small stylized tachibana (mandarin orange blossom) mon — the Ii clan crest — on the chest plate.
Often mounted on a dark bay or black horse fitted with matching red tack and lacquer.
Carries a yari (spear) or katana. His bearing radiates fierce discipline and barely-restrained aggression on the battlefield, contrasted with calm, composed authority off it.
