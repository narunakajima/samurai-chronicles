Kato Kiyomasa: tall, powerfully built Japanese samurai general, broad shoulders and a thick,
imposing frame — traditionally described as unusually tall for his day (said to be 6 shaku
3 sun, roughly 190 cm) and nicknamed the "demon general".
Face based on the surviving Honmyoji temple portrait: a long face, weathered and sun-darkened,
with thick dark eyebrows and small, narrow eyes set noticeably wide apart, holding a steady
unblinking gaze rather than a wide glare. Wears a full mustache and short beard.
Unmistakable silhouette: an extremely tall, black-lacquered eboshi-nari (long-eboshi) helmet
shaped like a folded court cap, rising straight up in a smooth trapezoidal peak roughly two feet
(over 60 cm) above his head — his signature, instantly recognizable headgear. A plain red sun
disc is painted on each side of the peak, and the front crest is a plain circular black-lacquered
plate (render it blank — no writing or characters of any kind).
Wears dark lacquered armor (gusoku) built for a large frame. Often carries his signature
jumonji-yari, a cross-bladed spear with a straight central blade and two curved crescent
side-blades.
Confident, grounded, powerful posture — radiates raw physical strength, battlefield nerve,
and blunt determination rather than elegance.
