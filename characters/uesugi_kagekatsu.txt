Uesugi Kagekatsu: serious, reserved male daimyo, adopted heir and successor of Uesugi Kenshin, shown from his teens to middle age across the episode.
Stern, square-jawed face, dark watchful eyes, taciturn expression — far less flamboyant than Kenshin but resolute and dutiful.
Wears black-lacquered armor prominently marked with the Uesugi "sasa rindo" (bamboo-and-gentian) crest.
Carries himself with quiet formality; rarely smiles. A leader who relies on trusted retainers rather than personal charisma.
