Shimazu Yoshihiro: powerfully built elderly male warrior in his mid-60s.
Weathered, deeply lined face with sharp, calculating eyes. Grey-black hair, stern jaw.
Black lacquered Sengoku-era armor bearing the Shimazu clan cross mon (十字紋) on the chest.
Stocky, compact frame that radiates physical strength even in old age.
Commands absolute authority; the presence of a man who has never doubted a decision in his life.
Often depicted mounted on a dark war horse. Carries a long naginata or katana.
