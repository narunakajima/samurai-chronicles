Naoe Kanetsugu: composed, intelligent-looking male samurai administrator and general, shown from his 20s to late 50s across the episode (predominantly mature, late 30s-50s).
Calm, sharp eyes; neat short beard; a dignified, watchful bearing — looks as much like a scholar as a warrior.
Wears dark indigo-grey lamellar armor marked with the Uesugi "sasa rindo" (bamboo-and-gentian) crest.
His kabuto helmet bears a large gilded character "愛" (Ai) mounted on the front crest — a Buddhist guardian-deity emblem, not a literal heart shape.
Often depicted holding a writing brush, ledger, or map alongside his katana — equally convincing commanding troops or balancing accounts.
Expression: controlled, calculating, rarely shows open emotion.
