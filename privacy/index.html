Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Samurai Chronicles ("this site") is the official website of an independently operated video series about Japanese history. This policy explains how information is handled on this site and in the tools used to produce and publish the series on YouTube.

2. Visitors to this site

This site only publishes static information. It does not use cookies that identify visitors, advertising trackers, or analytics tags, and it has no forms that collect names, email addresses, or other personal information.

Episode thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's image servers (img.youtube.com). These are image requests to Google's CDN; the operator of this site does not receive any information about individual visitors from them.

3. Use of Google user data (YouTube API Services)

The operator uses a private publishing tool that connects, through Google OAuth, to the operator's own YouTube channel (Samurai Chronicles). The tool is used only to manage that channel: uploading and scheduling videos, managing playlists, and reading and replying to comments on the channel's own videos.

Access is limited to the operator's own Google account. The tool does not access the accounts or private data of viewers or any other users. Authorization tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer; they are not transmitted to any external server and are not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. Comment text read by the tool is used only to write replies and is not stored or used for any other purpose, including advertising or training machine-learning models.

This tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the operator is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access that the operator has granted can be reviewed and revoked at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

5. Disclosure to third parties

Information obtained by the operator is not provided, sold, or lent to third parties for any purpose other than those described in this policy.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the law or in the services used. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
