Honda Tadakatsu: rugged male samurai warrior, late 30s to 60s depending on scene era.
Weathered, battle-hardened face with calm, intensely focused eyes and a thick, sturdy build.
Wears dark lacquered armor (gusoku) with bold geometric trim. Helmet crowned with large,
sweeping curved black deer antlers (kuwagata) — his unmistakable silhouette.
Always carries his legendary long-bladed spear, Tonbogiri ("Dragonfly Cutter"), with a
distinctively long, gleaming steel blade. Composed, disciplined posture — radiates quiet,
controlled strength rather than wild aggression. Never shown wounded or disheveled.
