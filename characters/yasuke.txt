Yasuke: a very tall Black African man in his late twenties (the chronicle of 1581 says he looked 26 or 27; the diary of 1582 gives his height as six shaku two bu, about 182 cm, roughly six feet, about a head taller than the Japanese men around him), with very deep dark brown-black skin ("black as ink" in the diary, "black as an ox" in the chronicle), broad shoulders, a deep chest and a powerful, athletic build (the chronicle credits him with "the strength of ten men"). He is NOT East Asian and NOT Japanese in appearance; he is the only person in the episode whose appearance is deliberately not normal for the setting. Show him clearly taller than the people next to him in every shared frame (his head level with the top of their heads plus a full head).
FACE (no portrait survives and no record describes his face; this is a designed look): a strong, calm, watchful face of a young man, broad nose, full lips, firm square jaw, dark steady eyes under a level brow; a short, neatly trimmed close beard along the jaw and chin with a short moustache (kept short and tidy so he still reads as a man in his twenties, not an older warrior). Expression composed and alert, never comic, never menacing. Prefer shots from behind, from a distance, in profile, or in deep shadow; keep any front-facing close-up to a minimum.
HAIR (no record; a designed look): short, close-cropped, tightly coiled natural black hair covering the whole scalp evenly, about one or two centimetres long. His forehead and crown are NOT shaved (no sakayaki) and he has NO chonmage topknot, NO dreadlocks, NO braids, NO bun or ponytail, unlike every Japanese man around him. Not a modern fade or styled cut: just short natural hair. Never a helmet, never a headband.
CLOTHING (an inference, not recorded; Japanese dress of a retainer in Nobunaga's household, not foreign dress): a plain dark indigo cotton kosode with a slightly lighter indigo hakama, tied with a plain dark sash; no crest, no armor, no cloak, no European clothing.
SWORD (recorded in one manuscript of the chronicle: a sayamaki short sword "noshi-tsuki", a gift from Nobunaga): one short hip sword about the length of a forearm, with no hand guard (no tsuba), its scabbard covered in a thin sheet of dull gold-coloured metal foil and a dark cord-wrapped hilt, thrust through his sash at the left front of his waist. Only this one short sword: no long katana, no spear, no club, no gun, no banner.
FEET: plain straw sandals (waraji) or bare feet.
Posture: upright, composed, deliberate, a man who knows he is looked at. Dignified and human in every shot: never a caricature, never a comic figure, never a monster, never a slave in chains, never a fantasy warrior in ornate armor.
