Mori Motonari: shrewd elderly male warlord, depicted mainly in his late 50s to 70s.
Weathered face with deep wrinkles, narrow calculating eyes, thin grey-white beard and mustache, grey-streaked hair often tied back or under a simple cap.
Wears dark grey or brown lacquered samurai armor with the Mori clan "ichimonji-mitsuboshi" crest (一文字三星 — one horizontal bar above three circles) on the chest and on banners.
Modest, understated appearance compared to flashier warlords — reflects his reputation for caution and intelligence over display.
Often shown studying maps, observing from a distance, or in quiet contemplation rather than charging into battle.
Carries a katana but is more often depicted in thoughtful or commanding poses than active combat stances.
