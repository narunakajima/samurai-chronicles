Maeda Toshiie: tall, broad-shouldered male samurai, Sengoku period (16th century Japan), famous for his exceptional skill with the spear (yari), later daimyo of Kaga.
As a young page/retainer (teens to 20s), wears simple but well-made armor, sharp-eyed and quick-tempered expression, often carrying an unusually long-shafted spear.
As a mature general (30s-50s), wears dark lacquered samurai armor with the Maeda clan crest (a stylized plum blossom / ume design known as "kaga umebachi") on the breastplate and banners, tall distinctive helmet.
In later-life scenes as Lord of Kaga (50s-60s), wears richly decorated armor and fine silk robes reflecting his immense wealth, dignified bearing, greying hair and beard.
Carries katana and signature long-shafted yari (spear) in battle scenes.
