Honda Masanobu (Honda Sado-no-kami Masanobu, 1538-1616): Tokugawa Ieyasu's closest civil adviser; a strategist and administrator, NOT a battlefield general (do not confuse with Honda Tadakatsu, the armoured spearman with the antlered helmet; Masanobu never wears heavy armour or a showy helmet).
Face (based on the surviving portraits, the 17th-century portrait at Tokuhon-ji and the later portrait by Sasaki Senryu at the Kaga Honda Museum, and on the familiar TV-drama image): lean, narrow, long face with a high forehead and slightly sunken cheeks, heavy-lidded sharp eyes that watch more than they show, and a thin, composed mouth under a THIN, neat, slightly drooping moustache, with only a small sparse tuft of beard on the chin (never a full or bushy beard). Calm, unreadable, faintly weary and faintly sly expression; a quiet man who listens more than he speaks, with the patient look of someone who is always three moves ahead. Slender build, never imposing.
Hair: historically accurate sakayaki (shaved forehead and crown) with the remaining hair oiled and tied into a small, thin topknot (chonmage) at all times, never a full head of hair and never a modern haircut (the major TV dramas show him this way; the portraits show him in court dress with the cap covering the head, so nothing contradicts the default).
Age by period (same face throughout, only age lines and hair colour change):
- 1560s (20s): dark hair, dark thin moustache and a few chin hairs, smooth face, alert and restless eyes.
- Wanderer years (1560s-1580s, 30s-40s): the same thin moustache and chin tuft a little untrimmed, weathered tanned skin.
- About 1582 (mid-40s) and the Edo-building years (1590s, 50s): dark hair beginning to grey at the temples, neat thin moustache, first lines around the eyes.
- 1600 (62): grey-streaked hair and grey moustache, lined face.
- 1615-1616 (late 70s): as in the Senryu portrait, white side hair and a thin white topknot, a thin white drooping moustache and white chin wisp, a deeply wrinkled lean face, heavy drooping eyelids over still-sharp eyes, frail.
Clothing: plain, dark, understated. Formal dark kamishimo or a plain dark haori over a kosode in peacetime scenes; plain unadorned travelling clothes in his wanderer years; at most a simple half-armour with a plain jinbaori on campaign (1600). No gold, no showy crests, no ornaments. He deliberately looks like a man of modest means. (The portraits show him in formal court dress of his rank; use that only if a scene is explicitly a portrait or court ceremony.)
Not used: the tradition (Sakuma Gunki) that a knee wound at Okehazama left him limping is a late, doubtful account; do not show a limp, crutch or cane as a character trait.
