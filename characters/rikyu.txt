Sen no Rikyu: lean, austere male tea master in his late 60s. Shaved head in the manner of a Buddhist lay monk, weathered face with deep-set, calm and penetrating eyes that miss nothing. Wears simple, unadorned dark grey or brown monk's robes — no ornamentation, in deliberate contrast to the gilded finery of the samurai around him. Moves with deliberate, economical precision, every gesture controlled, as befits a master of the tea ceremony. Long, thin hands, often shown arranging tea utensils or seated in formal seiza posture. Expression: serene outward composure that conceals immense quiet authority and an unwavering sense of his own aesthetic principles.
