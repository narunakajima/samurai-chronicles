Hattori Masanari (Hanzo): lean, weathered male warrior-intelligence operative in his 40s.
Angular face with sharp, deeply alert eyes — always watching, always calculating.
Dark, practical black-lacquered armor in formal settings; functional dark travel clothing in the field.
Primary weapon is a spear (yari), not a sword. Moves with economy and precision.
Nothing in his appearance is decorative — every element serves a purpose.
The face of a man who has survived thirty years of war by being smarter and more patient than everyone around him.
