Fuma Kotaro: tall, lean, imposing male ninja leader in his 30s-50s, hereditary title held by successive leaders of the Fuma clan rather than one fixed individual.
Weathered, deeply scarred face with a hard, unreadable expression — later legends exaggerate him into a giant with a monstrous, oni-like visage, but keep the base design human and grounded.
Dark, unadorned shinobi shozoku (ninja garb) in muted grays and browns, hood or dark cloth partially covering the lower face, no ornate armor or clan crests.
When his head is uncovered (no hood), his hair is always styled the same way: a full, unshaved head of hair simply pulled back and tied into a plain low ponytail/topknot at the back — deliberately NOT the shaved-forehead sakayaki style of formal samurai (a covert operative who disguises himself as a monk, merchant, or laborer would avoid the telltale samurai-class grooming mark). No bald forehead, no bangs falling loose, no modern haircut.
Carries concealed tools of infiltration — a short blade, coiled rope, small pouches — rather than an ostentatious weapon.
Moves and postures with predatory stillness; commands presence through quiet menace, stealth, and implied violence rather than open combat stances.
Historically accurate Sengoku-era ninja attire only — no European-style clothing, no modern hairstyles, no anachronistic fabric or dyes.
