Nogi Maresuke (1849-1912): Meiji-era Imperial Japanese Army officer, born in Edo to a samurai family of the Chofu domain (a branch of Choshu). Lost his regiment's flag in the Satsuma Rebellion (1877, age 27), commanded the Third Army at the Siege of Port Arthur (1904-05), head of the Gakushuin from 1907, died on the night of Emperor Meiji's funeral (13 September 1912). Many genuine photographs survive; the image most people know is the gaunt general with a close-trimmed grey-white beard in a dark braided army tunic and peaked cap. Appearance changes with the year, so match every scene to its date.

Face and build (constant): slender, upright, of average height, normal human proportions. A long, narrow, ascetic face with high, prominent cheekbones and lean cheeks (hollower with age), deep-set dark eyes under straight dark brows, a straight nose, thin lips firmly closed, a grave, controlled, rarely smiling expression.

HAIR (all scenes, all ages): Meiji-era short hair, cropped very close all over the head and fully covering the scalp. NO topknot, NO shaved crown, NO shaved forehead, NO sakayaki. Deliberately NOT the Edo-period samurai look. Hair colour by year: dark in 1877, iron-grey in 1904-05, white in 1907-1912.

1877 (age 27, army major, Satsuma Rebellion): young, lean face, dark close-cropped hair, a thin neat dark moustache with the chin and cheeks clean-shaven. Dark navy-blue Meiji army officer's tunic with horizontal black braid frogging across the chest and thin rank braid on the cuffs, dark trousers with a narrow red side stripe, a dark navy kepi with a red band, a Western-style officer's sabre on a belt, a long dark cape-coat in cold weather.

1904-1905 (age 55, full general, Port Arthur and Shuishiying): weathered, lean face, a grizzled grey-and-white full beard closely trimmed along the jaw and chin, a grey-white moustache, close-cropped iron-grey hair. Dark navy-blue general's tunic with horizontal black braid frogging, a dark peaked army cap with a red band, a sabre; in winter a long dark army greatcoat with a short shoulder cape.

1907-1912 (about 58-62, head of the Gakushuin): gaunter face, a close-trimmed white full beard and white moustache, white close-cropped hair. He wore army uniform even at the school: plain dark navy-blue army tunic with black braid frogging and a dark peaked cap.
