Young Miyamoto Musashi as a boy of about 13 to 16 (Azuchi-Momoyama, late 1590s to 1600): thin, wiry, tall for his age, sunburnt skin.
Wild, unkempt black hair, roughly tied back with a strip of cloth, loose strands falling over the forehead.
Worn, patched, dark hemp kimono, bare or straw-sandaled feet, no armor. A plain wooden staff or wooden sword at 13; a real short sword only from about 16.
Same face as the adult Musashi: angular, weathered features, sharp penetrating dark eyes with a hard, unblinking stare, a slightly heavy brow.
Body language: wary, coiled, self-contained, a child who has learned not to rely on anyone.
Crown never shaved (no sakayaki), a full untidy head of hair; later tradition attributes this to a scalp condition from childhood — do not depict any skin disease or scars.
Tall for his age anticipates the adult legend of about six shaku (later tradition, not a measured record); keep him a believable thin teenager, not a giant.
