Musashibo Benkei: enormous warrior monk, towering over other men by a full head.
Broad shouldered, barrel-chested, with a shaved head and fierce commanding face.
Wears black-lacquered armor over saffron monk's robes. Carries a massive naginata and seven weapons on his back.
Fierce loyal presence — fearsome to enemies, utterly devoted to Yoshitsune. Wild warrior's eyes.
