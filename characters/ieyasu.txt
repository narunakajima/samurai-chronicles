Tokugawa Ieyasu: stocky, powerfully built Japanese warlord in his 50s-60s.
Round face with deep-set watchful eyes that miss nothing. A traditional sakayaki (shaved forehead and pate) with the remaining greying hair oiled and tied back into a small samurai topknot (chonmage) — historically accurate Sengoku-period hairstyle at all times, never a full head of hair in a plain ponytail and never a modern haircut. Greying beard.
Wears black lacquered armor with gold accents. Calm, unhurried posture.
Expression of patient, calculating composure — a man who has survived everything.
