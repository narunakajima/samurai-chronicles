Sasaki Kojiro (Ganryu): lean, upright Japanese swordsman and fencing teacher in Kokura, about 1612.
Age deliberately ambiguous: the 1776 biography says eighteen, other readings make him much older; unless a scene prompt states an age, show a mature man whose age is hard to read (neither a smooth-faced youth nor an old man).
Hair: forehead and crown NOT shaved (no sakayaki, no topknot): long straight black hair drawn back and tied low at the nape, a few loose strands at the brow. Tidier than Musashi's untidy, loosely gathered hair.
Plain dark blue or indigo kimono and hakama; restrained, not flamboyant (the bright stage costumes come from later novels and films).
Carries an unusually long sword (nodachi), worn slung across his back or carried at his side, noticeably longer than a standard katana. This is his best-attested attribute.
Strong, composed, proud features; controlled expression; straight, disciplined posture.
Realistic and unglamorous: no makeup, not an idol-like pretty boy, no long flowing loose hair to the waist.
Note: no portrait of Kojiro survives. The tied-back unshaved hair and long sword follow the image fixed by later stage, novel, film and manga portrayals (Yoshikawa Eiji 1935, Vagabond, Fate); the beautiful young genius is a later picture, so do not stress youthful beauty.
