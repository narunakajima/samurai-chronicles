Sanada Yukimura: male samurai warrior, late 40s to early 50s.
Strong, lean build. Intense, dark eyes with calm determination.
Famous for brilliant crimson lacquered armor (red armor, "aka-zonae").
Helmet bears the six-coin crest (rokumonsenbukuro) in gold on black ground.
Long moustache and beard, weathered face of a man who has known exile.
Carries two swords and a long war spear. Commands absolute loyalty.
Projects quiet, unbreakable courage — the face of noble defeat.
