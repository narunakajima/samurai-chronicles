Oishi Kuranosuke: middle-aged male samurai in his mid-40s, unremarkable and mild-mannered in appearance at first glance — plain features, calm and unhurried demeanor, easy to underestimate. Beneath the mild exterior, sharp watchful eyes that reveal a formidable, patient intelligence. As Asano's chief retainer: simple but dignified dark kimono and formal robes, understated presence rather than showy authority. In disgraced-ronin scenes (Kyoto exile): deliberately shabby, disheveled clothing, a hint of theatrical carelessness masking total self-control. In the final raid: dark practical armor over black clothing suited for night combat, resolute and utterly composed expression.
