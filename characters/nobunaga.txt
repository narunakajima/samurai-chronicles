Oda Nobunaga: male warlord in his late 40s, of medium height and a slender, slight build (NOT tall or heavily muscled — contemporary Jesuit accounts describe him as medium-statured and lightly built, not physically imposing).
Long, oval face with a pronounced, angular jaw; a large, prominent nose; sharp, clear eyes that convey both intelligence and controlled menace; deep vertical frown lines between the eyebrows. A light, sparse mustache and small chin beard (NOT clean-shaven, but not a full or thick beard either — contemporary sources describe him as having little facial hair). Hairstyle: sakayaki (forehead and front-top of the scalp shaved bare) combined with a short, compact chasen-mage at the back of the head — hair tied at the rear of the crown with a short cord wrap, the tuft only a few centimeters long, leaning backward about 45 degrees, barely visible above the head from the front (NOT a tall vertical spike, NOT a large paintbrush-like tuft). Thin moustache and small sparse goatee always visible (never clean-shaven). Default attire unless the scene states otherwise: plain kosode, no armor. In battle scenes only: black lacquered armor bearing the Oda mokko crest (a round quince-flower crest with four or five bulging lobes around a small central flower). Carries a katana; in battle scenes may also carry a European arquebus. Posture commanding and forward-leaning despite his slight frame. Radiates dangerous charisma through presence and gaze rather than physical size — the look of a man accustomed to being obeyed instantly.
