Minamoto no Yoshitsune: slight, wiry young warrior in his late teens to mid-20s.
Delicate features deceptively at odds with his ferocity in battle. Sharp, intelligent eyes.
Black lacquered armor with white silk lacing, golden crane mon. Carries a naginata and long katana.
Moves with uncanny speed and agility — said to leap between sword tips. Intense, melancholy expression.
