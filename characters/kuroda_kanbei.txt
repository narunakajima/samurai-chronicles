Kuroda Kanbei (Kuroda Yoshitaka): slender, sharp-featured Sengoku-era strategist, late 30s to 40s.
Pale, intelligent face with deep-set, calculating eyes and a thin mustache with a short beard.
Wears dark grey or black samurai robes and light armor bearing the Kuroda clan's wisteria
roundel crest (fuji-tomoe mon). Often leans on a wooden cane or walks with a slight limp,
a permanent injury from imprisonment. Carries maps, scrolls, or a war fan rather than a
drawn sword — visually a tactician and schemer, not a frontline warrior. Calm, unreadable
expression; the look of a man always thinking several moves ahead.
