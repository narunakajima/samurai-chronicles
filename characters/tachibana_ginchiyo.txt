Tachibana Ginchiyo: slender, sharp-eyed female samurai commander, Sengoku period (16th century Japan), head of the Tachibana clan in Chikuzen province, Kyushu.
As a child (age 7), appears as a small girl in formal dark kimono, serious expression, often seated beside her father's retainers.
As a young woman (teens to 20s), wears practical dark blue-grey lacquered armor (do-maru) with the Tachibana clan crest (a stylized tachibana / mandarin orange blossom badge) on the breastplate, hair tied back, calm but resolute expression.
Often carries or stands near a matchlock musket (tanegashima) or naginata.
In quiet or indoor scenes, wears simple dark kimono with minimal ornamentation, posture upright and composed.
In later-life scenes, appears more weathered, hair greying, expression more weary but still dignified.
