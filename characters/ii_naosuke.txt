Ii Naosuke: Japanese daimyo and shogunate Chief Minister (Tairo), mid-40s, solid thick-set build, upright and disciplined posture.
Oval face, high forehead, cleanly shaven face with no mustache or beard (Edo-period daimyo custom), calm but piercing eyes with a stern gaze fixed on a single point, faint lines of stress and quiet resolve — matching his surviving portraits by Kano Eigaku and by his son Ii Naoyasu.
Hair worn in the traditional chonmage topknot with a shaved crown (sakayaki) — no Western-style haircut or cropped hair.
Wears formal kamishimo (kataginu winged vest and hakama) or a formal black/dark-indigo haori over hakama, muted colors befitting a senior Tokugawa shogunate official; the Ii family mon (heraldic crest) may appear subtly on fabric.
Carries a katana and wakizashi at his obi sash as befits his samurai rank, but his bearing reads more statesman than warrior — composed, watchful, resigned rather than aggressive.
No European clothing, no firearms as a personal weapon, no anachronistic hairstyles or accessories.
