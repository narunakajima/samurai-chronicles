Kusunoki Masashige: resolute male samurai commander, depicted in his late 30s to early 40s.
Lean, weathered face with sharp, intelligent eyes and a short dark beard.
Wears practical, lightly ornamented dark-lacquered samurai armor (less flashy than court nobles or famous daimyo) marked with the kikusui (chrysanthemum-on-water) crest on the chest and on banners — a mon awarded to him by Emperor Go-Daigo.
Calm, composed, and analytical demeanor — often shown studying terrain, maps, or fortifications rather than in dramatic combat poses.
Carries a katana and sometimes a war fan (gunbai) for command, but his presence emphasizes intellect and resolve over flamboyance.
