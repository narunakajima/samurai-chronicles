Takeda Shingen: broad, powerfully built Japanese warlord in his 40s, the Tiger of Kai.
Wide, commanding face with a thick beard. Sharp calculating eyes.
Distinctive dark lacquered armor with a crimson fur cloak (jinbaori) draped over his shoulders.
Carries a war fan (tessen) and katana. Often depicted seated in command.
Projects absolute authority and strategic intelligence. A man who views all situations as problems to be solved.
Takeda clan diamond-pattern (Takeda bishi) visible on banners and armor.
Kenshin's greatest rival — equally matched in military genius, opposite in philosophy.
