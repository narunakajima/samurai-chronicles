Takeda Shingen: broad, powerfully built Japanese warlord in his 40s, the Tiger of Kai.
Wide, commanding face with a full thick beard and heavy brows. Sharp calculating eyes.
Tonsured: he took Buddhist vows in 1559 and "Shingen" is his monastic name, so from this point
on his head is clean-shaven in the Buddhist manner (no chonmage, no shaved-pate topknot).
Sometimes wears a dark monk's headcloth or a helmet with a white hair mane; bare shaven head otherwise.
Distinctive dark lacquered armor with a crimson fur-trimmed cloak (jinbaori) over his shoulders.
Carries a gunbai — a solid lacquered command fan with a short handle, NOT a folding paper fan — and a katana.
Often depicted seated on a camp stool (shogi) in command.
Projects absolute authority and strategic intelligence. A man who views all situations as problems to be solved.
Takeda clan diamond-pattern (Takeda bishi) visible on banners and armor.
Kenshin's greatest rival — equally matched in military genius, opposite in philosophy.
