Shima Sakon: powerfully built veteran samurai general, appearing in his mid-to-late 50s.
Weathered, battle-scarred face with a thick grey-streaked beard and stern, deep-set eyes.
Broad shoulders and a thick neck — the physical bearing of a lifelong frontline commander, not a courtier.
Wears dark iron-plated okegawa-do armor over a faded, battle-worn cloth undergarment, with a simple weathered sashimono banner on his back.
Hair tied back in a simple practical topknot, no ornamentation.
Carries a well-used katana and often a war fan or spear.
Expression of grim, unshakeable resolve — the calm of a man who has already made peace with death.
