Kira Yoshinaka: elderly male shogunate official in his early 60s, refined and fastidious in bearing, accustomed to deference. Thin, sharp features with a faint permanent expression of condescension. Wears rich formal court robes appropriate to a master of ceremonial protocol (koke), fine silk in muted, expensive colors, hair immaculately groomed. Moves with precise, deliberate formality. In later scenes, increasingly wary and watchful, a man aware — but not quite believing — that danger might still be coming for him.
