Minamoto no Yoritomo: heavyset, stocky man in his late 30s to 40s. Calculating, cold demeanor.
Square jaw, thick neck, steady unreadable eyes. Black formal court robes or dark battle armor.
Imposing stillness — commands through authority and intimidation rather than charisma.
Carries himself with the deliberate weight of a man who lets others do the fighting while he controls the outcome.
