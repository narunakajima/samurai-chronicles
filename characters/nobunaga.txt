Oda Nobunaga: male warlord in his late 40s, of medium height and a slender, slight build (NOT tall or heavily muscled — contemporary Jesuit accounts describe him as medium-statured and lightly built, not physically imposing).
Long, oval face with a pronounced, angular jaw; a large, prominent nose; sharp, clear eyes that convey both intelligence and controlled menace; deep vertical frown lines between the eyebrows. A light, sparse mustache and small chin beard (NOT clean-shaven, but not a full or thick beard either — contemporary sources describe him as having little facial hair). Period-accurate chonmage (topknot with shaved pate). Black lacquered armor with golden paulownia crest mon. Carries a European arquebus as well as a katana. Posture commanding and forward-leaning despite his slight frame. Radiates dangerous charisma through presence and gaze rather than physical size — the look of a man accustomed to being obeyed instantly.
