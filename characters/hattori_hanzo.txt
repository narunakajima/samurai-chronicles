Hattori Hanzo: lean, middle-aged male ninja commander in his late 40s. Sharp angular face, watchful dark eyes that register everything and reveal nothing. Wears dark practical clothing — a blend of light armor and shinobi garb, functional over ceremonial. Moves with complete economy, never a wasted gesture. Carries a tanto and shuriken; weapons are invisible until needed. Expression: perfectly unreadable — the face of a man trained since childhood to show nothing. The Tokugawa clan crest barely visible on dark fabric.
